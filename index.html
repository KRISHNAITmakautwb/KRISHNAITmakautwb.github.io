Engineer Profile & Research Affiliation

I am an AI Security and Cryptography Engineer based in India, actively contributing as a Project Associate at IIT Bhilai. My research is anchored around the FinTeQ initiative, funded by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Govt. of India.

My engineering philosophy revolves around verifiable, mathematically sound security. In an era where quantum computing threatens classical asymmetric cryptosystems (RSA, ECDSA), I build systems that transition financial frameworks to NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography (ML-DSA / Dilithium, Falcon, Kyber) integrated with hardware security tokens via native C PKCS#11 implementations.

Concurrently, I investigate vulnerabilities in advanced AI architectures — from cryptanalytic extraction of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) neural models through query access, to building sovereign local LLM infrastructure (SurrealDB, FastAPI, Ollama) and type-safe deterministic autonomous agent dispatchers.

Core Technical Directives: Resilience to Shor's algorithm • Zero external cloud leaks for confidential LLM inferences • Strict adherence to ISO 32000-2 & PAdES cryptographic specifications.
