About

Security you can verify

I'm an AI-security and cryptography engineer at the de.ci.phe.red Lab, IIT Bhilai. I work in the FinTeQ initiative, funded by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Govt. of India, which builds trustworthy security for financial systems.

On the cryptography side I work with hardware tokens through PKCS#11, digital signatures (CMS / PKCS#7, X.509) and PDF signatures under ISO 32000 and PAdES. In the optics lab I run a B92 QKD demonstration and characterise an entangled photon source.

On the AI side I study how much of a model an attacker can recover just by querying it, from Mixture-of-Experts gating to the weights of ordinary neural networks. I also build local, air-gapped AI tools that keep confidential data off the cloud.

Currently working on

  • Neural-network extractionRecovering a model's weights through adaptive queries, treating it like a cryptographic oracle.
  • TrusMeetLab video meetings unlocked by a C-DAC TrusToken USB key, with signed meeting minutes.
  • B92 QKD demonstrationA working B92 quantum key distribution setup, with time-tagged photon detection and QBER analysis.
  • Entangled photon sourceBuilding the EPS hardware setup and measuring its polarization visibility.

LLM inferenceOpen source

RIC: Router → Inference → Cache

An LLM gateway that sends each query to the cheapest model that can answer it. Short or simple questions go to a local Gemma model on Ollama, demanding ones go to a cloud model through OmniRoute, and repeated questions are answered from a SQLite cache. It includes a FastAPI server and a benchmark comparing local, cloud and routed modes.

Quantum key distributionResearch project

B92 protocol demonstration

An experimental B92 quantum key distribution setup. Alice sends single photons in two non-orthogonal polarization states (0° and 45°), and Bob measures in two non-orthogonal bases. An FPGA time tagger records detector clicks and coincidences, from which the sifted key and the quantum bit error rate (QBER) are worked out.

QKD · FPGA time tagger · PyQt6Time Tagger app (Ubuntu & Windows) · code on request
Entangled photonsResearch project

EPS hardware setup & analysis

Hands-on work on the lab's entangled photon source (EPS): setting up the optics, detectors, half-wave plate and motorised rotation stage, then analysing singles and coincidence counts to fit the polarization correlation curve and measure its visibility.

Quantum optics · Coincidence counting · Data analysisLab project · no public repo
Quantum opticsOpen source

EPS Visibility

Measures the polarization visibility of an entangled photon source: reads live counts from a Qutess FPGA time tagger, steps an Arduino rotation stage, and reports raw and fitted visibility with its uncertainty. Ships with a hardware simulator and tests.
