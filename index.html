01.

About Me

I am an AI Security and Cryptography Engineer passionate about developing robust, high-assurance digital infrastructure for real-world applications.

Currently, I serve as a Project Associate at IIT Bhilai, actively contributing to the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) funded FinTeQ project. My core focus lies in the convergence of post-quantum cryptography (PQC), hardware security tokens (PKCS#11, smartcards), and high-integrity financial transaction verification.

Beyond cryptography, I actively research and construct private, privacy-preserving AI systems: deployable local LLM architectures (Ollama, vLLM), autonomous AI agent workflows with type-safe dispatchers, and cryptanalytic model parameter recovery methods.

Research Mission: Bridging quantum-resilient cryptography with autonomous agent architectures to ensure uncompromised privacy, verifiability, and security.
